# STORYBOARD — 17 Horizontal Screens

The story is a playful fake-breakup opening followed by a keepsake of small moments. Each screen should be readable at a glance. Use the full-screen copy below as the default; keep optional micro-annotations short.

## 01 — The mock breakup

**Headline:** I'M DONE WITH YOU, TAMATAR. 🍅

**Copy:** Seriously, idhuku mela ennala mudila jii. Pathutu reply eh panna maatengra… verum “Mm”, “Hmm”, “Mm okayy” nu dry ah thatti vidura. Seri. Let's end this right here.

**Art cue:** Minimal cream paper, one tiny tomato-red mark, serious editorial typography, a discreet envelope/seal. Do not make it genuinely threatening or sad.

## 02 — The twist

**Headline:** HAHAHA, U THINK I DO?

**Punchline:** VAAIPE ILA PO! 🤭

**Art cue:** Flip the mood with a playful scribble, sticker-like tomato, and a hand-drawn arrow.

## Story pacing

- Screens 01–02: fast comedic setup and reveal.
- Screens 03–08: playful observations and the meetup memories.
- Screens 09–10: emotional pause.
- Screens 11–14: daily rituals, games, teasing, and tiny nonsense.
- Screens 15–16: deeper care and shared future-facing memories.
- Screen 17: quiet, affectionate close.

## Copy integrity

Keep the quoted Tanglish lines recognizable. Light copy-editing is allowed only for fit and readability. Never fabricate a new quote and attribute it to Thabbu. If a line is paraphrased, do not use quotation marks.
